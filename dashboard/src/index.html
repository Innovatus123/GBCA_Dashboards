      Definitions

      Member count
      Current members of the Active, Associate and Affiliate membership types in GrowthZone, from the weekly engagement run. The tile opens the GBCA Member Map.
      Engaged, Low Risk, Risk
      Each member is scored weekly on GBCA's 2026 Active, Associate or Affiliate engagement scorecard from GrowthZone data for the trailing 12 months. Engaged combines Highly Engaged and Engaged (a score above 75 for Active and Associate members, above 65 for Affiliates). Risk combines Moderate and High Risk (40 or below). Low Risk is everything between. The full report in OneDrive keeps all five bands.
      Net Promoter Score
      Percent promoters (9-10) minus percent detractors (0-6) on "How likely are you to recommend GBCA membership to a peer firm?" Rolling 90 days.
      Value-to-dues multiple
      Value delivered year to date divided by dues recognized year to date (annual dues pro-rated by week), so the ratio is comparable week to week.
      Cost avoidance
      A conservative estimate of cost a member did not incur because GBCA resolved the issue, recorded with its basis. Guidance-only resolutions count toward volume but carry $0.

      About this dashboard

      Live: the member count and section 1.0, rebuilt every Sunday night from the latest GBCA Member Engagement Report (GrowthZone, refreshed Saturday night).

      Sample: sections 2.0 to 4.0 are still layout data. They are not GBCA actuals and should not be quoted; their weeks are aligned to the live calendar for layout only.

      Weeks run Monday to Sunday (ISO weeks). Issue resolution (4.0) reports by calendar month; the month in progress is month to date and compares with the same days of the prior month. Targets shown are placeholders pending leadership sign-off.
